Featured · Annual-style event report

LCOY Nepal 2026 Event Report

Nepal’s national youth climate conference, endorsed by YOUNGO, brought together 177 young people from all seven provinces. The report had to work for two audiences at once: funders and ministries who need outcomes, and young delegates who need to see themselves in it.

Format
43 pages, A4 portrait
Role
Layout, typesetting, data graphics, photo editing
Audience
Funders, partners, government, youth
Year
2026

What I did

  • Built a repeatable page system: section openers, a two-column body grid, photo-led programme spreads and a consistent running footer.
  • Turned the participant survey into clear demographic charts and a visual timeline of the consultation process.
  • Designed a partner wall that gives more than 40 organisations equal, tidy visual weight.
  • Ended with a full-bleed social page (#lcoynepal2026) so the report could be shared online.

    03 — How I work

    Calm process, editable files, no surprises.

    1. 01

      Listen & audit

      I read last year’s report, the brand guidelines and the new copy first, and agree which parts of the brand should stay and which should grow.

    2. 02

      Structure the story

      A flatplan and page system come before any polish: where the stats land, which stories get spreads, how funders scan it.

    3. 03

      Design in rounds

      I share work in clear feedback rounds, with async updates that fit a team spread from the UK to Southeast Asia.

    4. 04

      Hand over cleanly

      You get a print-quality PDF and organised, fully editable Canva files with named pages, locked grids and reusable templates.

    Tools

    Canva (professional, editable handover) · Adobe Photoshop · Video editing · Microsoft Office
