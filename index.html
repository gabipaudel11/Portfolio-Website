(02) Case study

LCOY Nepal
2026

The national youth climate conference, endorsed by YOUNGO. 177 delegates from all seven provinces and more than 2,000 young people in the wider consultation. The report went to ministries, funders and the delegates themselves.

I set the grid, typeset all 43 pages, drew the charts from the survey data and built the partner pages so forty-odd logos sit evenly.

(05) About

Ganesh Paudel. Social work student at Tribhuvan University. Designer by habit, organiser by trade.

Most of my design work started because someone needed a poster by Friday. That turned into reports, then into whole event identities. I work in Canva and Photoshop and hand over files the team can keep editing after I leave.
